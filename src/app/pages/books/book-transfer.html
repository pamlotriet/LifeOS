Export all books

The JSON backup includes every book, category, copy format, reading progress, dates, ratings, review, cover link, favourite and mood/genre tags. It exports the full library, including books hidden by filters.

Use JSON to restore or edit and re-import your library. CSV is a summary for spreadsheets and cannot be imported here. Cover images are saved as links.
